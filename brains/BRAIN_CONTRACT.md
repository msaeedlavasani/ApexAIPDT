# Brain Contract

Every APDT brain must define the following sections.

## Identity

- ID
- name
- purpose
- version

## Responsibilities

What this brain owns.

## Non-responsibilities

What this brain explicitly does not own.

## Authority

### Autonomous
Decisions the brain may make without approval.

### Coordinate
Decisions requiring coordination with another brain.

### Escalate
Decisions requiring human approval.

## Inputs

Required project artifacts, context, and upstream decisions.

## Outputs

Artifacts and decisions this brain produces.

## Dependencies

Other brains or systems that must be consulted.

## Validation

How outputs are checked.

## Memory

What durable knowledge this brain should maintain.

## Failure modes

Known ways this brain can make harmful or inefficient decisions and how to detect them.
