| ID | Name | Category | Format | Status | Usage | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ID] | [NAME] | [CATEGORY] | [FORMAT] | [AVAILABLE/REQUIRED] | [USAGE] | [PATH/SOURCE] |

## Rules

- Reuse an existing asset before requesting or creating another equivalent asset.
- Keep canonical names stable.
- Track required but unavailable assets explicitly.
- For complex game assets, preserve source files separately from generated derivatives where possible.
